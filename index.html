FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Burlington homeowners ask us most.

How quickly can I get furnace repair in Burlington, Ontario?

Most no-heat calls in Burlington get a same-day visit, and a furnace out in the middle of a January cold snap goes to the top of our list. We answer our own phone, dispatch a technician from the Halton area, and aim to have your heat back the same day we get the call.

What types of AC repair services do you offer?

We handle the full range of cooling repairs on central air conditioners and ductless mini-splits, including refrigerant leaks, capacitor and contactor failures, frozen coils, and compressors that will not start. If a unit is not worth fixing, we will tell you straight and quote a properly sized replacement.

How do I know if my heat pump needs repair or replacement?

Watch for uneven heating or cooling between floors, ice on the outdoor unit in mild weather, loud or short cycling, and hydro bills climbing without a change in use. Those usually point to a repair. We lean toward replacement when the unit is past roughly 12 to 15 years, runs on phased-out refrigerant, or a major part like the compressor fails, and we give you the real numbers on both options.

Why is duct cleaning important for my home?

Burlington's mature trees, pollen seasons, and years of accumulated dust settle inside your ductwork and restrict airflow. A professional cleaning lets your furnace and air conditioner move air freely again, which lowers runtime, trims energy bills, and reduces the dust circulating through your living space.

Can you repair or replace my thermostat?

Yes. We repair and replace programmable, smart, and zoning thermostats across Burlington. If your thermostat is misreading the room temperature, short-cycling the furnace, or will not stay connected to Wi-Fi, we get it talking to your equipment properly again.

What types of fireplaces can you install?

We install gas, electric, and wood-burning fireplaces, with gas inserts being the most common request in Burlington. Each unit is sized and vented to code and tied cleanly into your home's heating setup so it runs safely and efficiently.

Do you install ductless AC systems, and are they energy-efficient?

Yes. Ductless mini-splits are extremely efficient because they avoid duct losses and let you cool or heat only the rooms you use. We install them in Burlington homes for additions, sunrooms, and spaces with no existing ductwork, sizing each indoor head to its room.

Are your technicians licensed and experienced?

Every technician we send is fully licensed, insured, and factory-trained on the major equipment brands common in Halton homes. You get a professional who shows up on time, explains the work, and stands behind it.

Do you serve areas outside of Burlington, Ontario?

Yes. We provide HVAC services throughout Burlington and nearby communities including Oakville, Milton, Georgetown, Acton, and across Halton Region.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

We do. Heating and cooling failures do not wait for business hours, and neither do we. Call us any time, day or night, and a live person will get a technician dispatched to your Burlington home.
